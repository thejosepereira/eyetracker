What you're seeing
Move your head toward and away from the screen. As the estimated distance changes, the engine recomputes the eye's blur for that distance and re-renders the correction. Farther away → the model predicts more blur → stronger pre-distortion. Set your screen's real PPI for the distance-to-blur mapping to be meaningful.
Experimental, not a medical device. Distance is estimated from iris spacing (~11.7 mm human iris / ~63 mm IPD), good to roughly a centimetre after calibration — not a depth-sensor measurement. A native iOS app can read the TrueDepth IR sensor directly (ARKit) for higher accuracy. All processing is local; nothing is uploaded.
