VisionCorrect — Self-test

Change your prescription and look at the pre-distorted image with your own eyes.
How to test: take your glasses/contacts off, cover one eye, and hold the screen at the distance you set below. Then flip correction ON/OFF (button or space). With the right settings, ON should look clearer than OFF for that eye. Set your real screen PPI and distance so the blur matches your eye.

Calibrate your screen

Hold a real credit / ID / bank card flat against the screen and drag the slider until the outline matches the card's width exactly. (All such cards are the same size: 85.6 mm wide.)
