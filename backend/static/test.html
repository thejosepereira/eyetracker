VisionCorrect — Human Test Harness

Blinded, 3-condition letter-identification study. A = original · B = contrast-reduced only (control) · C = full correction.
Before you start: take glasses/contacts off, cover the eye you are not testing, and keep the screen at the fixed distance below. Test one eye at a time using that eye's prescription.
Experimental technology, not a medical device. Aim to set the letter box so your uncorrected (condition A) accuracy lands around 50–70% — too easy or too hard leaves no room to detect a difference. Randomise ON/OFF is handled for you (conditions are shuffled and never shown during the run). For anything beyond informal friends-and-family testing, obtain ethics/IRB review and involve a vision-care professional.
