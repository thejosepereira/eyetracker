VisionCorrect — standalone

Runs entirely in your browser. No server, works offline. Correction, eye simulation and the human test are all computed locally.
Experimental technology, not a medical device and not a replacement for prescribed eyewear. Panels 2 & 4 simulate the prescription so you can judge the effect on a normal screen.
Before you start: glasses/contacts off, cover the eye you are not testing, keep the screen at the set distance. Test one eye at a time with that eye's prescription.
Three conditions per trial (shuffled, never shown): A original · B contrast-reduced only (control) · C full correction. The meaningful result is C − B. Set the letter box so condition A accuracy is ~50–70%. Beyond informal testing, obtain ethics/IRB review and a vision professional.
